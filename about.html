About House of Blends

A coffee and all-day dining cafe in Wright Town, Jabalpur. Sip. Eat. Bliss. That's the whole idea.

How it started

A cafe built around one idea.

House of Blends opened in Wright Town with a simple goal: make a cafe in Jabalpur where the coffee is actually worth the wait, and where the food menu is deep enough that regulars keep finding new dishes months in. We are a unit of R&K Hospitality, and everything on the menu, from the espresso to the pizza dough, is made to the same standard.

The name says it plainly. Sip something good. Eat something better. Let the room do the rest. We built House of Blends to be the kind of place you come to for a quick coffee and end up staying two hours, because nobody is rushing you out.

The coffee

Ground to order, poured slow.

Every cup starts with beans ground fresh behind the counter, not the night before. Our baristas pull each espresso shot to order and steam milk in small batches, which is why a cappuccino here takes a minute longer than the instant version down the road. We think that minute is the point.

From a straight Americano to a Hazelnut Mocha, the hot and cold coffee menu runs long, alongside frappes, shakes, mocktails, and cold-pressed iced teas for the days you want something that isn't coffee at all.

Why people stay

It isn't just the coffee.

A menu that doesn't repeat

Breakfast, toast, steaks, Maggi, sandwiches, burgers, pizza, pasta, pancakes, rice and noodles, plus the full drinks list. Come weekly and you'll still be finding things.

A room built for staying

Wood and black metal furniture, a wall that says Sip, Eat, Bliss in hand-lettered script, and a patio out front for the evenings. Nobody is watching the clock.

Open every day, all day

12:30 PM to 10:00 PM, Monday through Sunday. Breakfast at 1, dinner at 9, or just a coffee in between. We're set up for however you use the day.
